靳若春，国防科技大学计算机学院副教授，硕士生研究生导师，爱丁堡大学信息学博士（数据库方向），**研究方向为数据库理论与系统、大数据、大模型数据管理**。融合逻辑规则和统计学习方法，围绕大规模图数据质量管理、图与关系数据统一查询、并行图计算优化、大模型数据管理等课题开展研究，近年来在SIGMOD、VLDB、ICDE、TODS等CCF-A类数据库顶级会议与期刊发表学术论文18篇，以第一完成人授权/申请专利6项，主持国自科青年基金项目等。CCF高级会员，担任CCF数据库专委会委员、CCF信息系统专委会委员，CCF软件工程专委会委员，获2024-2026“CCF青年人才托举工程”（中国科协第十届青年人才托举工程项目支持）。主讲本科专业必修课《数据科学与大数据基础》。

Jin Ruichun, Associate Professor and Master’s Supervisor at the College of Computer Science and Technology, National University of Defense Technology. He received his PhD in Informatics (Database) from the University of Edinburgh. His research interests include database theory and systems, big data, and data management for large language models. He integrates logical rules and statistical learning methods, conducting research on topics such as large-scale graph data quality management, unified querying over graph and relational data, optimization of parallel graph computing, and data management for large language models. In recent years, he has published 18 academic papers in top database conferences and journals (CCF-A) including SIGMOD, VLDB, ICDE and TODS. He holds/has filed 6 patents as the primary inventor and presides over projects including the NSFC Young Scientists Fund. He is a Senior Member of CCF, and serves as a member of the CCF Technical Committee on Database, the CCF Technical Committee on Information Systems, and the CCF Technical Committee on Software Engineering. He was selected for the 2024–2026 CCF Young Elite Scientists Sponsorship Program, supported by the 10th Young Elite Scientists Sponsorship Program of the China Association for Science and Technology (CAST). He teaches the compulsory undergraduate core course Fundamentals of Data Science and Big Data.

所在课题组长期招收硕士、博士研究生，诚邀对数据库、大数据管理、大模型等研究方向感兴趣的同学加入，有意者请将个人简历及自我介绍发送至我的邮箱。
